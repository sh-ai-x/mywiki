# AI Engineer (facet)

> **You're an AI Engineer if**: you build production AI / agent systems. Day-to-day you make decisions about frameworks (LangGraph vs CrewAI), RAG architectures, evaluation harnesses, observability, deployment infrastructure, and the agent-computer interface.

This is the broadest of the AI tracks — it overlaps with both [[../ai-applied-engineer/_index|AI Applied Engineer]] (more product-focused) and [[../ai-native-developer/_index|AI-Native Developer]] (more generalist).
