# AI-Native Developer (facet)

> **You're an AI-Native Developer if**: you're a software engineer who uses AI as the primary collaborator in your dev loop. You write code, but most of your commits are produced by an agent (Claude Code, Codex, etc.) with you as the editor. The "AI-native" framing means you *build with* AI rather than just *use* it.

This is the most generalist of the AI tracks. An AI-Native Developer is closer to a "10× SWE" than to an ML researcher.
