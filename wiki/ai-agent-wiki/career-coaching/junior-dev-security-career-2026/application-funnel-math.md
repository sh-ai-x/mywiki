# Application funnel math

> **TL;DR**: 2026 baseline: ~1–2% cold-apply → offer; 5–10% with strong referrals (3–5× multiplier on the recruiter-screen step). Cold applications are the worst channel; warm intros + a strong portfolio is the highest. 신입 공채 vs 상시채 are different processes; internship-to-FT conversion is the strongest single path for current students.

(Pane C owns the deep funnel math. This section is the operational checklist.)

**The 2026 funnel (KR SWE 신입, observed baseline):**

- **Apply → Recruiter screen:** ~5% (industry-aggregated for SWE; higher for AI/security skills). Cold applications are the *worst* channel.
- **Recruiter screen → Tech phone:** ~30–40%. Resume strength + role-fit is the gate.
- **Tech phone → Onsite:** ~40–60%. Performance on the phone screen + role-fit.
- **Onsite → Offer:** ~20–40%. Multi-round performance + culture fit.
- **Overall apply → offer:** ~1–2% baseline; ~5–10% with strong referrals.

**Referral multipliers (the 2026 math):**

- A referral increases the recruiter-screen pass rate by **3–5×** (employee referrals bypass the cold-application queue; the resume reaches a human reviewer directly).
- A referral at a 신입 공채 (대기업) is often a *requirement* for the first screen at all — without an internal reference, the resume may not be read at all.
- A "warm introduction" via LinkedIn to a hiring manager or recruiter has roughly the same effect as a formal referral, and is often faster to obtain.

**Channel mix that works in 2026:**

- **Direct-apply via Wanted** ([wanted.co.kr](https://www.wanted.co.kr/)) — Korea's tech-focused job board; most startup roles post here.
- **Saramin** ([saramin.co.kr](https://www.saramin.co.kr/zf_user/)) — broadest KR job board; 대기업 / 중견 / 공공 all post here.
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply** — multinational roles; increasing in importance in 2026.
- **Direct company career pages** — every 대기업 has one; 신입 공채 cycles are time-bounded.
- **Referrals / warm intros** — the highest-conversion channel; budget at least 30% of effort here.
- **Campus recruiting** (for current students) — the dominant channel for 신입 at 대기업.
- **Hackathon / CTF as recruitment channel** — Codegate, Samsung CTF, Toss Hackathon — many companies recruit actively from these.

**신입 공채 vs 상시채:**

- **신입 공채 (대기업):** batch hiring, usually 1–2 cycles per year (spring for summer start, fall for spring start). Process: 서류 → 인적성 → 직무면접 → 임원면접 → 처우협상. Conversion is high (1–5% of applicants get offers) because the process is structured.
- **상시채 (startups + 중견):** rolling hiring, immediate start, faster process. Conversion is variable but the per-application ROI is higher because there's less competition per slot.
- **2026 specific:** many 대기업 reduced the size of their 2026 신입 공채 cohort; the slots that did exist were heavily backfilled from 미들급 lateral hires. Startup and public-sector hiring is *up* relative to 2024.

**Internship-to-full-time conversion:**

- The strongest single path for a current student is **summer-internship → return offer**. Conversion rates at Korean 대기업 are 50–80%; at startups, 30–60%.
- For a junior already graduated: the next-best signal is **contract / freelance → conversion**. Many Korean startups hire 신입 via a 3–6 month contract with a conversion gate at the end. The conversion rate is 40–70%.
