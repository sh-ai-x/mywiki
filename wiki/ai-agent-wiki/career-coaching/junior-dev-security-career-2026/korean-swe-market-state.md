# Korean SWE market state (2026-Q3)

> **TL;DR**: 2026-Q3 is the toughest junior SWE market in over a decade — postings down ~73% YoY, big-tech 신입 share collapsed from 32% (2019) to 7%, and AI-tool fluency is now the single mandatory screening filter. Salary bands: ₩48–58M (대기업 신입), ₩38–48M (중견), ₩36–46M (스타트업 base).

The 2026-Q3 market is the toughest for juniors in over a decade. The post-2022 "tech hiring bubble" has fully deflated; 2026 is the second year of structural normalization, and entry-level pipelines are the most exposed part.

**Headline numbers (2026-Q3):**

- Junior developer job postings down **~73% YoY** by March 2026 (Instagram/JobKorea data — see [JobKorea report](https://www.jobkorea.co.kr/goodjob/tip/view?News_No=22611)). The fall accelerated in Q2 as 2025 미들급 graduates failed to clear the market, freeing up slots that historically would have been reserved for 신입.
- Big-tech share of new-grad hires fell from **32% in 2019 to 7% in 2026** (per [큰돌의 분석](https://blog.naver.com/jhc9639/224185240319)). Big-tech (네카·카·당·쥐, SKY-affiliated groups, 대형 포털) is doing roughly 1/4 of the new-grad intake it did at peak.
- JobKorea's 2026 하반기 report shows: **75.8%** of employers say postings are down YoY, **48.4%** say they have headcount but aren't filling (TO 있어도 안 뽑음 — the market is selective, not capacity-limited), **39.5%** are explicitly requiring AI skills, **36.8%** report competition per slot has intensified.
- AI skills (not AI engineering — just "uses an AI tool effectively") is the single new mandatory ask in postings that did go live; postings without it are screened out before the recruiter reads them.

**Salary bands for junior SWE (2026-Q3, in KRW millions, base + typical bonus):**

| Segment | 대기업 (NAVER/Kakao/Coupang/Samsung) | 중견 (mid-cap) | 스타트업 (series B+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 신입 (0 yrs) | 48–58M | 38–48M | 36–46M (options excluded) |
| 1년차 | 52–62M | 42–52M | 40–52M |
| 2–3년차 (주니어) | 58–72M | 48–62M | 46–62M |
| Stock / options (startup only) | n/a | n/a | 0.05–0.30% vesting |

These figures are base + typical bonus, excluding stock. Top-tier 신입 at NAVER/Kakao/Coupang can clear 60M with sign-on + 첫해 특별 bonus; the [Outsourcing.co.kr commentary](https://www.outsourcing.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=91132) pegs the typical "liveable" senior-engineer range at 60–80M for the same period. The 6,000만 floor is a 신입 benchmark that increasingly separates top-quartile from median.

**What changed since 2024:**

- 2024 was a correction year; 2025 was the year 신입 postings froze. 2026 is the year they fell off a cliff.
- AI-as-a-tool is now mandatory for the interview loop itself (live-coding + AI-assist is increasingly common).
- The "AI replaces junior engineers" narrative has reached executives — postings are dropping partly because AI productivity gains are being used to delay hiring, not to reduce work.
- The market has bifurcated: top schools + visible side projects + AI fluency get interview calls; everyone else competes in a much larger pool with much lower conversion.

**Top employers by tier (Q3 2026):**

- **대기업 (still hiring but smaller classes):** 삼성전자 (DS부문 + SDS), NAVER, Kakao, Coupang, SK C&C (SK그룹 IT), LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever.
- **중견:** NHN (LINE Plus successor), Toss (Viva Republica), 두나무 (Upbit), 쏘카, 마이리얼트립, 직방, 당근마켓 (Karrot), Hyperconnect.
- **스타트업 (active hiring, equity-driven):** Upstage, Scatter Lab, Riiid, Midas Labs, Krafton (게임 특화), Superb AI, Nota AI (AI-on-device), SelectStar (SRE/security).
- **Public sector:** ETRI, KISTI, NIPA (AI/barometer of state demand), 행정안전부 (정부24), 한국인터넷진흥원 (KISA).

The public-sector angle is underused by juniors — KISA and NIPA are stable employers, pay 35–45M for 신입, and don't require the same brand-name pedigree as 대기업. Worth a section in the action plan.
